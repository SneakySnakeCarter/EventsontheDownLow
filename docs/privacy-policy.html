Privacy Policy for EventsontheDownLow

Last updated: 3 Aug 2026

EventsontheDownLow ("the app") is a personal event and reminder tracker. This policy explains what information the app handles and — just as importantly — what it does not collect, store remotely, or share.

Summary

EventsontheDownLow does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal data to the developer or to any third party. Everything you create in the app — event titles, notes, dates, and photos — stays stored locally on your own device.

Information We Do Not Collect

The app does not collect, and has no ability to transmit:

  • Your name, email address, or any account information (there is no account or sign-in of any kind)
  • Location data
  • Analytics or usage tracking of any kind
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Any data shared with third parties, advertisers, or data brokers

The app contains no analytics SDKs, no advertising frameworks, and makes no network requests to any server operated by the developer or anyone else.

Information Stored Locally On Your Device

Event titles, notes, dates, recurrence settings, reminder times, and any photos you attach are stored only in the app's private local storage (an on-device database and file storage), using Apple's standard application sandbox. This information:

  • Never leaves your device unless you explicitly choose to (see "Save to Photos" below)
  • Is not accessible to the developer — we have no way to see, access, or retrieve it
  • Is deleted permanently if you delete the app, unless it was included in your own device backup (see below)

Camera Access

If you choose to attach a photo to an event using the "Take Photo" option, the app requests access to your device's camera at that moment. Photos taken this way are saved only within the app's own local storage, not to your system Photos library, unless you separately choose to save them there (see below). The app cannot access your camera at any other time.

Photo Library Access

The app can let you choose an existing photo to attach to an event using the system photo picker. This picker runs as a separate, privacy-preserving system component — the app only ever receives the specific photo you pick, and is never granted general access to browse your photo library.

Separately, if you tap "Save to Photos" on an event's picture, the app requests add-only permission — a narrower permission that lets it add that one photo to your system Photos library, but never lets it read, browse, or access any of your existing photos.

Notifications

If you enable notifications, the app schedules reminders locally on your device for your events, using Apple's on-device notification system. These reminders are scheduled and delivered entirely on your device — no reminder content is sent to, or generated by, any external server.

Device Backups

If you use iCloud Backup or a local computer backup (e.g. via Finder) for your device, the app's local data — including event details and photos — may be included in that backup, the same as any other app's data. This is a feature of your device's own backup system, controlled entirely by you and Apple's iCloud/backup infrastructure, not something the developer has access to or control over.

Children's Privacy

EventsontheDownLow is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13 — consistent with the rest of this policy, since the app does not collect information from anyone at all.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy is ever updated, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will change accordingly. Continued use of the app after a change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
